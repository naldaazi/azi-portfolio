A calendar built around priorities

Full of meetings, no focus blocks, no buffers, and no sense of which week mattered.

Google CalendarCalendly
The problem

The calendar reflected everyone else's requests. Back-to-back calls filled the day, focus work got pushed to evenings, and priorities were invisible until they were urgent.

What I did
01Rebuilt the week around the founder's actual goals rather than incoming requests.
02Colour-coded meetings by category so the week reads at a glance.
03Protected focus blocks and added buffer time between calls.
04Consolidated meetings into predictable windows instead of all-day scatter.

Result

A calendar that shows its priorities before you open it, with focus time that survives the week.
