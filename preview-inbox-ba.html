An inbox that stays actionable

Hundreds of unread emails, no labels, no priority — important messages buried under noise.

GmailGoogle Workspace
The problem

Every message landed in one place with no system behind it. Decisions were delayed because the message that mattered sat underneath forty that didn't.

What I did
01Triaged the backlog into categories that matched real decisions, not generic folders.
02Built labels by category, priority flags, and filters that keep the structure intact.
03Wrote one triage rule: every email is actionable in under 30 seconds.
04Set a repeatable daily sweep so the inbox never rebuilds itself.

Result

An inbox that stays organised after the clean-up — important threads surface first, and nothing sits unread for days.
